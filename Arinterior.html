AR experience

What happens after "View In AR."

Plane detection — finds the floor automatically, no markers needed.
Real-scale placement — furniture appears at its true size in the room.
Drag furniture — move any piece to a different spot without leaving AR.
Rotate furniture — turn a piece to match the room's layout.
Material switching — change fabric or finish while it's standing in the room.
Screenshot capture — save the placed view to share or send for a quote.
Works today on Android (Scene Viewer / WebXR). iPhone's AR Quick Look needs a .usdz file alongside the .glb — add one once your models are ready.
